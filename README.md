Make the AI judge feel curious. Your program writes one text (a short text written to intrigue the AI judge reading it), an AI judge compares the texts two at a time, and the 20 best win rates share $50.

The entry is a single 200-character text: a door that opens between two sentences, and the one question waiting behind it — written to intrigue, in plain concrete words, with nothing forced.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
